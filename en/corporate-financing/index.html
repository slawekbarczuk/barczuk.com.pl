01

Financing needs and structure

The analysis covers capital requirements arising from investment projects, day-to-day operations and the working-capital cycle. Potential sources, financing tenors and repayment schedules are assessed against the company's cash flows.

Support in determining an appropriate mix of equity and debt and preparing alternative financing structures.

Illustrative deliverable: Assessment of financing needs and alternative structures.

02

Financial modelling and transaction preparation

Financial models cover projections of profit and loss, balance sheet and cash flows, assessment of debt-servicing capacity and the impact of changing assumptions on liquidity and financial ratios.

Preparation includes the information and materials needed to present the company or investment project to prospective lenders.

Illustrative deliverable: Financial model, projections and scenario analyses.

03

Financing process and banking relationships

Support covers preparing and coordinating the financing process: from defining its scope and timetable, through engaging selected institutions, to reviewing proposals and negotiating terms.

This also includes helping prepare responses to lenders' questions and organising the required data and documentation.

Illustrative deliverable: Information package, process timetable and comparison of proposals.

04

Financing terms and bank security

Analysis and support in negotiations with banks cover pricing, tenors and repayment arrangements, drawdown conditions and covenants.

Support includes designing and negotiating a security package that meets lenders' requirements while taking account of the company's financial capacity and future funding needs.

Bank guarantees. Support in obtaining bank guarantees includes selecting the appropriate type, preparing the application and agreeing the amount, term, wording and security with the bank. Ongoing support may cover amendments, extensions and release or expiry of guarantees.

Syndicated financing. Support in coordinating participating banks covers shared terms, collateral arrangements and information undertakings. Documentation matters requiring legal advice are handled in conjunction with the company's legal advisers.

Illustrative deliverable: Analysis of loan terms, proposed security package and negotiation issues list.

05

Investment financing and staged drawdowns

Support with financing construction, expansion and commencement of operations, including manufacturing facilities.

The financing structure is assessed in relation to the capital expenditure budget, equity contribution, expenditure schedule and the ramp-up period to planned operating capacity.

Coordination covers preparing documentation and fulfilling obligations relating to subsequent drawdowns and the administration of funding.

Illustrative deliverable: Schedule of conditions, drawdowns and settlements.

06

Debt monitoring and amendments to terms

Ongoing support includes monitoring financial covenants, reporting obligations, repayment dates and other undertakings to lenders.

Support covers preparing reports, updating forecasts and analysing variances. Where the company's circumstances or an investment project call for changes, discussions are prepared on credit terms, security, maturities or refinancing.

Illustrative deliverable: Compliance calendar, monitoring and amendment scenarios.
